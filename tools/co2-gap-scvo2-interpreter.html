CO2 Gap & ScvO2 Interpretation

Tier-gated interpretation across ΔPCO2, the Pv-aCO2/Ca-vO2 ratio, ScvO2, and lactate. Population-aware thresholds. Decision-path trace for trainees.
Educational tool — not for clinical decision-making. This is a teaching aid for reasoning about CO2-derived indices. It is not a diagnostic device, is not FDA-cleared, and has not been prospectively validated. Every threshold here is an adjunct to the bedside picture, and several are extrapolated from adult data or unsourced in the population they are applied to — each one is labelled in the decision trace and in the population tooltip. Defer to clinical judgment, the treating team, and local protocols. Do not enter protected health information — all values stay in this browser session and are never saved or transmitted.
Tier 1 (mandatory)
mmHg
mmHg
Sampling constraint. Every threshold in this tool assumes a central venous sample — SVC or right atrium, from a correctly sited CVC — drawn at the same time as the arterial sample and analysed immediately. A peripheral venous gas gives a systematically wider, uninterpretable gap that none of these cut-points apply to. Bakker’s 6 mmHg was derived from mixed venous blood through a pulmonary artery catheter. On ECMO: draw from the patient’s own central line or the pre-oxygenator (drainage-side) port — a post-oxygenator sample has already been decarboxylated and makes the gap meaningless.
Tier 2 (hemodynamic)
%
Percent, not a fraction — enter 72, not 0.72. In cyanotic, single-ventricle, or shunt-dependent physiology the 70% cut-point does not apply; after a bidirectional Glenn an SVC sample reflects cerebral, not global, extraction.
Tier 3 (metabolic)
mmol/L
mmol/L. A result reported in mg/dL must be converted before it is entered — a mg/dL figure read as mmol/L routes the case as severe anaerobic metabolism.

Edge warnings

Enter paired PaCO2 and PvCO2 to begin. The CO2 gap is a flow-marker, not an oxygenation-marker. Tier 1 unlocks adequacy of flow; tier 2 separates macro- from micro-hemodynamic patterns; tier 3 layers anaerobic metabolism.
