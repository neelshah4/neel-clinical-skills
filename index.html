Critical care teaching tools

Move the inputs, watch the physiology

Twelve interactive tools for pediatric and adult critical care. Each is a single self-contained page with no install, no account, and no server. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

v2.x · updated 9 October 2026 · CC BY-NC 4.0
These are teaching tools, not medical devices. None is externally validated or FDA-cleared, and none is for autonomous clinical decision-making. Thresholds here are risk-stratifiers drawn from specific populations, not treatment targets. Defer to your own judgment and local protocols, and do not enter protected health information. See the full disclaimer.
  1. 01

    V-V ECMO Oxygenation Calculator

    ECMO / gas exchange

    Solves the venovenous mixing equation in both directions. Enter a cardiac output and it predicts arterial saturation; enter a measured SaO2 and it solves for cardiac output. Inputs are sorted into measured, set on the pump, assumed and solved, and you can check the model against a measured SaO2, a cardiac output or the circuit’s pre-oxygenator saturation. Recirculation, VO2 and hemoglobin are inputs; oxygen delivery is an output. Five worked scenarios load with one click, and the page runs offline.

    v3.0 · updated 2026-10-09 · Open tool
  2. 02

    CO2 Gap and ScvO2 Interpreter

    Shock / perfusion

    Reads PaCO2, PvCO2, ScvO2 and lactate through three stacked tiers: the venoarterial gap, the macrocirculatory versus microcirculatory pattern, and anaerobic stratification. Returns a verdict, the edge cases that invalidate the gap, and the next clinical actions, each traced to its source.

    v2.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  3. 03

    ICU CBC Interpreter

    Sepsis / infection

    Bedside CBC interpretation through a sepsis lens with the evidence graded per finding. Nine confounders can be switched on, each of which changes what the count means: steroids, G-CSF, recent chemotherapy, asplenia, hematologic malignancy, a seizure within 24 hours, late pregnancy, a beta-agonist infusion, and recent transfusion.

    v2.3 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  4. 04

    Pediatric AKI Risk Tool

    Renal

    Renal angina index tracked day by day against its threshold, with a Bayesian layer that moves a pretest severe-AKI rate to a posttest probability using urinary NGAL and cystatin C. Derived likelihood ratios are labelled as derived rather than presented as directly reported.

    v2.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  5. 05

    Albuterol Delivery During HFNC

    Asthma / aerosol

    Charts inhaled lung dose as a percentage of the nominal nebulizer dose against high-flow nasal cannula flow, so the tradeoff between flow and delivery is visible rather than asserted. Names the platforms (Optiflow and Vapotherm) and states which nebulizer each takes and where it sits in the circuit, and works peak inspiratory flow through six cases from infant to adult so the flow-to-demand ratio has a number behind it. Values that come from adult studies are flagged as adult-derived where they appear.

    v3.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  6. 06

    PRx, CPPopt and the MAP Challenge

    Neurocritical care

    Cerebral autoregulation made concrete for fellows: animated pressure-reactivity waveforms, a PRx versus CPP U-curve you can move, a norepinephrine and MAP challenge simulator, and a walkthrough of the tiered protocol with its safety limits.

    v2.5 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  7. 07

    The Venous Return Wars

    Hemodynamics

    An interactive teaching essay on the Guyton and Brengelmann dispute, worked from the ground up. Four live rigs let you drive the argument instead of reading it: two tanks and a pump, a venous return curve you can reshape, a vasodilate-then-defend-blood-pressure sequence, and a fourth that separates what you changed, the contents or the container, from what the change became, flow or pressure.

    v2.4 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  8. 08

    Critical Asthma: Drugs, Noninvasive Support, and the Ventilator

    Asthma / ventilation

    Ten agents in one pharmacology table that shows where its sources disagree rather than averaging them, a toxicity matrix for what stacks when three drugs run at once, an interactive Campbell diagram for CPAP and BiPAP, and two PEEP schematics built from adult physiology that is labelled as adult. A ledger at the end names every claim that is adult, modelled, or nobody's data.

    v2.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  9. 09

    Ventilator Fundamentals

    Respiratory mechanics

    The equation of motion, made movable. Pressure, flow and volume are solved live rather than drawn, so changing compliance or resistance redraws real curves and the difference between a resistance problem and a compliance problem becomes something you can see. Covers holds and what each one measures, volume control against pressure control and PRVC, trigger types, and how inspiratory time, rise time and cycle-off actually work.

    v2.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  10. 10

    Ventilator Advanced

    Patient effort / gas exchange

    What the fundamentals leave out. Patient effort graphed against ventilator pressure and how the two sum; why PRVC withdraws support as the patient works harder while pressure control holds it constant, and why that can hide a deterioration; the decremental trial that finds best-compliance PEEP; the recruitment-to-inflation ratio and the airway opening pressure, each worked through as a bedside procedure and computed from the numbers the manoeuvre gives you; and deadspace against shunt, which separates a CO2 problem from an oxygenation one.

    v2.2 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  11. 11

    ARDS Inflammatory Subphenotypes

    ARDS / exploratory

    Exploratory only, and it says so before you type anything. Runs the published hypo- and hyperinflammatory classifiers that actually print their coefficients, refuses to run the ones that do not, and reports a range rather than a number when an input is missing. Every pediatric result carries how poorly the adult signature transports to children, and the calculator opens on one estimate per model with the model bench a click away.

    v2.3 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool
  12. 12

    Pediatric Dysnatremia and Diabetes Insipidus

    Endocrine / sodium

    Two tabs for one clinical episode. The first reads sodium, weight-indexed urine output and urine concentration into a ranked differential, says out loud when the data cannot separate SIADH from salt wasting, and names the next test rather than guessing; the confounders that decouple specific gravity from osmolality change the reading rather than sitting in a footnote. The second runs a vasopressin infusion in mU/kg/hr against live charts, with the dose flags marked as this unit’s figures because no source names them.

    v1.1 · updated 2026-10-03 · Open tool

Every quantitative claim in these tools went through a literature refresh and was then reviewed independently by readers who did not write the file. Identifiers are resolved against PubMed and Crossref rather than recalled, and a sample of each tool's verified identifiers is re-fetched blind and compared before release.

Some numbers here never had a citation: consensus thresholds, likelihood ratios derived from a pooled AUROC, delivery percentages synthesized across bench studies. Those are kept and labelled as derived rather than deleted or dressed up as directly reported. Where a tool says a number is an approximation, believe it.

Found an error? Open an issue. Corrections to clinical content are the most useful contribution anyone can make here.
